Why lightweight wins

Speed earns enquiries

Slow sites lose people before they see a thing. Lean pages keep them - and load fast on regional connections.

Less to break

No plugins, databases or page-builder cruft. Fewer moving parts to go wrong - and a far smaller target for attackers.

Cheaper to run

Static hosting costs cents, not hundreds a year. No subscriptions quietly stacking up.

Easy to keep current

Updates are quick edits, not mini-projects. Your site stays fresh without the upkeep bill.
