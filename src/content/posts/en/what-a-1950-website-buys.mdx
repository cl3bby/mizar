People ask what a website costs before they ask what is in it, which is fair enough. So here is the honest version of what the Essential package includes.

## The work

Essential is A$1,950. For that you get a designed and built site of up to about five pages: home, services, about, contact, and one or two extras where they make sense.

That covers the structure, the layout, the copy editing, the mobile version, and the technical setup. It also covers the boring parts that nobody sees — page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, and a Search Console setup so the site can actually be found.

## What is not included

It is not a logo design, a brand identity, or a photo shoot. If you do not have decent photos, I will tell you what to take and how, but I am not turning up with a camera.

It is not an online store, a booking system, or a customer portal. Those are separate builds with separate quotes.

It is not endless revisions. The quote sets out what is in scope. Small changes during the build are normal and expected. Rebuilding the whole direction halfway through is a different conversation.

## Why that number

A site like this takes roughly two to four weeks of actual work, spread around the other things a solo operator has to do. The price reflects the build, the setup, and the fact that you deal with the same person from the first email to the handover.

You own the finished site. Hosting and the domain stay in your name. If you want someone to keep an eye on it afterwards, Website Care is A$69 a month or A$199 a quarter.

If you want more pages, more custom design, or a more involved build, Signature is A$2,750. Either way, you get a written quote before anything starts.
