Wilko's Building is a concept redesign for a regional construction company. The existing site carried a lot of content but made it hard to tell what the business actually does or how to start a conversation.

The redesign leads with completed work, groups services into plain-language categories, and puts an enquiry path on every screen. Mobile performance was the priority — most of this traffic arrives from a phone.
