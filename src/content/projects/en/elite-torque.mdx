Elite Torque Mechanical is a concept redesign for a regional workshop. The brief was simple: make the services obvious, make the phone number easy to find, and make the page load before a customer gives up.

The layout is mobile-first, the service list is restructured around what people actually search for, and the build is static so there is no database or plugin layer slowing it down.
