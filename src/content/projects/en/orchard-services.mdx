Orchard Services is a concept remake of an outdated site for a respected horticultural consultancy. The content was strong; the presentation was not.

The rebuild keeps the expertise front and centre, replaces the heavy legacy stack with a static build, and gives the service pages a clear structure that both customers and search engines can follow.
