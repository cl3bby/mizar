Wall's Sand & Gravel is a concept redesign for a family-run quarry. Quarry sites often bury the useful information — products, sizes, delivery areas — under stock photography and generic copy.

This version is deliberately minimal: the product range is the hero, the contact details are always one tap away, and the whole thing loads as a static site with compressed images.
