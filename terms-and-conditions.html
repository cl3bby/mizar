Quotes and scope

Every project starts with a free chat and a written quote. Quotes are valid for 30 days and state the agreed scope - pages, features, and what's included in the chosen package (Essential or Signature). Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before it's done, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Accepting a project

A project begins when you accept the written quote or proposal and the required deposit has been received. The quote, agreed scope and these terms together form the agreement for the project. If there is any conflict, the written quote or proposal takes priority for project-specific details.

Payment

Projects are invoiced in Australian dollars; invoices will state any applicable GST. Payment is 50% deposit before work begins and 50% after final approval, before the site goes live. The final domain cutover and launch don't happen until the final payment is received. Domain registration and other third-party costs are billed at cost. Bank transfer is the usual payment method. Website Care is A$69/month (or A$199/quarter), billed in advance, and can be cancelled anytime - it runs to the end of the paid month. Work outside a package or Care plan is charged at A$95/hour in 30-minute increments, or quoted for larger pieces.

Cancellation

You can cancel a project at any time by letting me know in writing. If you cancel after work has begun, amounts already paid may be retained to cover work completed, time reserved and reasonable costs incurred up to the date of cancellation. If the value of work completed exceeds the amount already paid, I may invoice the difference. Any amount paid for work that has not been performed or costs that have not been incurred will be dealt with in accordance with applicable law.

Client delays and inactive projects

Project timelines rely on you providing content, access and feedback when reasonably requested. Delays on your side may move the delivery date. If a project is inactive for more than 30 days because required material or feedback has not been provided, I may pause the project and reschedule the remaining work. If it remains inactive for more than 60 days, I may close the project and invoice for work completed to that point. Restarting a closed project may require a new timeline or quote.

Revisions

Each package includes the revision rounds stated on the pricing page (two for Essential, three for Signature). A revision round is consolidated feedback on the current design - not a new direction. Beyond the included rounds, extra revisions are charged at the hourly rate or quoted before proceeding.

Timelines

Timelines are agreed in the quote and depend on photos, content and feedback arriving when promised. If something on your side delays things, no stress - the timeline just shifts accordingly, and I'll keep you posted.
